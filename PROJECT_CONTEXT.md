# Project Context: Kopi Kita

Kopi Kita is a warm, calm coffee shop website.

## Pages

- `/`: landing page — make visitors interested and guide them to menu or booking.
- `/menu`: product list — help visitors discover drinks and pastries.
- `/booking`: booking form — make booking details easy to submit.

## Visual Identity

- Background cream: `#FAF3E0`
- Main text and buttons: dark brown `#4A2C2A`
- Accent: warm orange
- Overall feeling: warm, calm, modern, and not tacky.
- Keep the design consistent across all pages.
