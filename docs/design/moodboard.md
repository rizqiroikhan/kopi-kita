# Moodboard — menu dan ordering

Referensi dipakai untuk mempelajari prinsip, bukan menyalin tampilan. Screenshot disimpan pada 12 Oktober 2026.

## 1. Blue Bottle Coffee — landing/menu
![Blue Bottle](moodboard/01-blue-bottle.jpg)
Source: https://bluebottlecoffee.com/us/eng  
**What's good:** the restrained palette and generous empty space keep one content group dominant instead of making every card compete.  
**Principle:** hierarchy and spacing.

## 2. Sweetgreen — menu
![Sweetgreen](moodboard/02-sweetgreen.jpg)
Source: https://www.sweetgreen.com/menu  
**What's good:** food choices use repeatable card structure, so title, supporting detail, and action can be scanned in a predictable order.  
**Principle:** hierarchy and spacing.

## 3. Airbnb — search/booking entry
![Airbnb](moodboard/03-airbnb.jpg)
Source: https://www.airbnb.com/  
**What's good:** the search control groups related decisions in one bounded surface while separating it from surrounding promotional content.  
**Principle:** spacing and depth.

## 4. Starbucks — menu
![Starbucks](moodboard/04-starbucks.jpg)
Source: https://www.starbucks.com/menu  
**What's good:** the brand accent is reserved for meaningful actions while the content hierarchy relies mainly on scale and contrast.  
**Principle:** color and hierarchy.

## 5. Pret A Manger — food and drink landing
![Pret](moodboard/05-pret.jpg)
Source: https://www.pret.co.uk/en-GB  
**What's good:** high-contrast product imagery is paired with concise labels, making products recognizable before a visitor reads long copy.  
**Principle:** hierarchy and color.
